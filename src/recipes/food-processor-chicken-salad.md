Creamy chopped chicken salad with red onion and kosher dill, made almost entirely in the food processor.

## Ingredients

### Main Ingredients
- 3 cooked chicken breasts
- 1/2 red onion
- 1 large kosher dill pickle
- 3/4 cup mayonnaise

### Seasoning
- 1/2 teaspoon kosher salt
- 1/4 teaspoon black pepper
- 2 teaspoons lemon juice

## Instructions

1. Put the cooked chicken breasts in the large food processor bowl with the standard blade. Pulse until finely chopped - short bursts, not a puree. Transfer to a separate mixing bowl.
2. Without washing the processor bowl, add the red onion, kosher dill pickle, and about half of the mayonnaise. Run it until the onion and pickle are chopped small and suspended in the mayo, scraping down once.
3. Spoon the onion-pickle mixture into the chicken a little at a time, folding as you go, until the salad is creamy. Add more of the remaining mayonnaise if it still looks dry.
4. Season with the kosher salt and black pepper, then add the lemon juice to brighten it. Taste and adjust - pickle brine works in place of lemon if you'd rather.
5. Cover and refrigerate at least 30 minutes so it firms up and the flavors settle.

## Notes

- Poached or rotisserie chicken both work; make sure it's fully cooled before processing or the mayo will loosen.
- The mayonnaise amount is a starting point - chicken breast varies a lot in moisture, so add the last quarter cup only if you need it.

## Time Information

- CHILL TIME: 30 minutes (minimum)
