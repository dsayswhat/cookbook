## Ingredients

### Main Ingredients
- 1 3/4 cups (12 ounces) sugar
- 1/4 teaspoon Diamond Crystal kosher salt (half as much if iodized)
- 1/2 cup (2 1/2 ounces) cornstarch
- 1/2 cup (5 ounces) egg yolks (from about 8 large eggs)
- 3 tablespoons lemon zest (from 3 large or 6 medium lemons)
- 1 cup (8 ounces) freshly squeezed lemon juice (from 4 large or 8 medium lemons)
- 2 1/4 cups (18 ounces) water
- 1/4 teaspoon orange flower water (optional)

## Instructions

1. Combine sugar, salt, and cornstarch in a 3-quart stainless steel saucier, then whisk in egg yolks, lemon zest, lemon juice, and water.
2. Cook over medium-low heat, whisking gently, until the custard is steaming hot, about 5 minutes (135°F).
3. Increase heat to medium and continue cooking until the custard is thick, about 3 minutes more.
4. Once it begins to bubble sluggishly, whisk 2 full minutes; this is important to neutralize a starch-dissolving protein found in egg yolks.
5. Remove from heat and strain through a fine-mesh sieve into a bowl, pressing with a flexible spatula to push the custard through.
6. Stir in orange flower water, if using.
7. Scrape into a serving dish or storage container, spread into an even layer, and cool until a skin forms over the surface, about 30 minutes.
8. Wrap in plastic and refrigerate until needed, up to 24 hours.

## Notes

- You must use nonreactive equipment throughout this recipe, or the custard will develop a harsh metallic flavor. Prime culprits can include some old-fashioned whisks and sieves.
- This custard works as a filling for lemon meringue pie or can be enjoyed on its own.

## Time Information

- ACTIVE TIME: about 1 hour
- DOWNTIME: 4-hour refrigeration, plus 1 hour to cool
- YIELD: Enough custard for one 9-inch pie or 12 servings
