## Ingredients

### Wet Ingredients
- 1 cup sourdough starter
- 1 cup milk
- 1 egg
- 2 tbsp vegetable oil

### Dry Ingredients
- 1 cup flour
- 1 or 2 Tbsp sugar
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1  tsp baking soda
- 1 tsp baking powder

## Instructions

1. preheat griddle to 375°F
2. mix wet ingredients
3. mix dry ingredients
4. add wet to dry
5. adjust consistency with a little more milk
6. pour about 1/4 cup of batter per pancake onto the griddle
7. flip when bubbles form on top and the edges look set, about 1–2 minutes per side
